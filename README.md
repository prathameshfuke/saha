SAHA is an agentic AI meeting assistant built for institutions that demand accountability, clarity, and action from every meeting — whether in a physical room or on Google Meet.
SAHA listens to every participant, understands the agenda, and works silently in the background. By the time the meeting ends, every decision has been captured, every task has been assigned, and every person has a personalised document waiting for them.
The live transcript is sealed, timestamped, and admin-only — so nothing said in a meeting can ever be denied.
What SAHA does:

Reads and understands the agenda before the meeting begins
Live transcription with per-speaker attribution
Works in physical rooms (via app) and Google Meet
Auto-generates Minutes of Meeting (MoM)
Personalised post-meeting doc per participant — tasks, deadlines, responsibilities
Tamper-proof transcript with admin-only access for non-repudiation
AI Q&A — ask anything about any past meeting instantly
PDF output with secure cloud storage and full audit logs
